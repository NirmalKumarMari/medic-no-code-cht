 Ministry of Public Health, Kingdom of Westvale (fictional)

 Job Aid: Newborn Danger Signs Checklist
 Print and keep in the CHW visit bag. Laminated card, side 1 of 1.


 TICK ANY BOX THAT APPLIES
   [ ] Baby is not feeding well
   [ ] Baby has had convulsions (fits)
   [ ] Breathing: 61 or more breaths in one minute
   [ ] Severe chest in-drawing
   [ ] Baby does not move on its own
   [ ] Temperature 37.6 °C or higher (fever)
   [ ] Temperature 35.4 °C or lower (too cold)
   [ ] Yellow skin/eyes and baby is under 24 hours old
   [ ] Yellow palms and soles (any age)

   Result                                   What to do

   ONE OR MORE boxes ticked                 REFER to the health facility now for further evaluation

   NO boxes ticked                          Routine postnatal care. Next visit as scheduled (24 h, day 3, day 7–14,
                                            week 6).

 Temperatures are read to one decimal place (0.1 °C).
