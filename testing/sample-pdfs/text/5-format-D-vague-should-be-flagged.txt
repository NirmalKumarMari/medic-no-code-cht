 Danger signs
 Health workers should refer newborns who are breathing fast, who have a high fever, or who feel cold to the
 touch. Babies with early jaundice should also be referred, as should babies who are not feeding properly.

 Babies who seem unwell in other ways may need referral depending on the health worker's judgement.

 Follow-up
 Referred babies should be followed up soon after referral.
