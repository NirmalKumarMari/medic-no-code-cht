 Ministry of Health, Republic of Examplia (fictional)

 Community Newborn Visit Protocol
 Protocol EX-MNH-07, edition 3. Applies to community health workers at every postnatal home visit.


 1. Visit schedule
   Visit                      When

   Visit 1                    Within 24 hours of birth (or before facility discharge)

   Visit 2                    Day 3 (48 to 72 hours after birth)

   Visit 3                    Between day 7 and day 14

   Visit 4                    Week 6 after birth


 2. Danger sign check (Table 2)
 Check every sign at every visit. If ANY sign in Table 2 is present, refer the newborn to a health facility for
 further evaluation. If no sign is present, give routine postnatal care.

   Sign                         Danger if                                                            Action

   Feeding                      Not feeding well                                                     REFER

   Convulsions                  Any history of convulsions                                           REFER

   Breathing rate               More than 60 breaths per minute                                      REFER

   Chest                        Severe chest in-drawing                                              REFER

   Movement                     No spontaneous movement                                              REFER

   Temperature (high)           Above 37.5 °C                                                        REFER

   Temperature (low)            Below 35.5 °C                                                        REFER

   Jaundice                     Any jaundice when the baby is less than 24 hours old                 REFER

   Palms and soles              Yellow palms and soles, at any age                                   REFER

   None of the above            —                                                                    Routine postnatal care
